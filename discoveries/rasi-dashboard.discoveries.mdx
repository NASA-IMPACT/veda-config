    ## Project Information:

    RASI LIS Dashboard is a JupyterHub-based analysis tool for assessing climate-scale changes in water supply and demand in the upper Rio Grande basin. Information is based on land surface simulations forced by multiple hypothetical climate outcomes.

    Practical Goals are to increase preparedness for changes in water availability, and provide technical information for infrastructure and policy decisionmaking.

    ## Technical Goals are to…
    Identify processes that most strongly influence streamflow trends
    Characterize the impact of soil moisture deficit on runoff generation
    Understand how variability in monsoon timing and intensity affect water supply in the Rio Grande Basin
    Model how drought risk changes under climate change scenarios.

    Simulations are run with 4 CMIP6 future greenhouse gas emission scenarios (shared socioeconomic pathways; SSPs), which are produced as part of the NASA Earth Exchange Global Daily Downscaled Projections dataset.

    The computational timestep used by Noah-MP is sub-daily, so data are disaggregated with the MERRA-2 hourly reanalysis

    Funding provided by NASA Western Water Applications Office (WWAO).
