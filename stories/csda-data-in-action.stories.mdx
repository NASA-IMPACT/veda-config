  ### What can we see from space?

  What does catastrophic tornado damage actually look like from satellite imagery?

  The interactive map below allows you to directly compare conditions before and after the violent May 21, 2024 EF-4 tornado in Greenfield, Iowa.

  Use the slider to explore how the landscape was drastically altered.

  ### What to look for

  As you move the slider, focus on three key features:

  - **Damage Path:** A continuous swath cutting across the landscape  
  - **Surface Changes:** Areas where vegetation and structures have been removed or altered  
  - **Urban Impact:** Concentrated destruction within the town of Greenfield  

  </Prose>
</Block>

<Block>
  <Prose>

  ### What the imagery reveals

  The contrast between the two images highlights how effectively high-resolution commercial satellite data captures tornado damage.

  In the post-storm scene:

  - Vegetated areas appear stripped or discolored  
  - Buildings and infrastructure show clear signs of removal or damage  
  - The tornado track becomes sharply defined across both rural and urban areas  

  These patterns allow us to map the spatial footprint of the tornado directly from space.

  </Prose>
</Block>

<Block>
  <Prose>

  ### Why this matters

  High-resolution commercial satellite imagery such as this Planet data enables rapid and consistent analysis of tornado impacts.

  It can be used to:

  - Map damage extent shortly after an event  
  - Identify regions of more intense damage  
  - Support comparisons with ground surveys and radar observations  

  This data is a valuable and key part of understanding how tornadoes interact with the landscape and built environment.

  </Prose>
</Block>

<Block>
  <Prose>

  ### Event Overview

  The May 21, 2024 Greenfield, Iowa tornado was the strongest of the year, reaching EF-4 intensity with estimated peak winds of 185 mph.

  The tornado tracked across southwestern Iowa for approximately 44 miles, producing widespread destruction across multiple counties. In Greenfield, numerous homes were completely destroyed, resulting in several fatalities and numerous injuries.

  </Prose>
</Block>
