  ### About

  This dataset contains monthly land surface parameters simulated by the Noah 3.6.1 model in the Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET) Land Data Assimilation System (FLDAS). Each file represents how the month's total evapotranspiration compares with the 35-year monthly climatology from 1982 to 2016.
  
  The data are provided at 0.1 degree resolution with global coverage from 60S to 90N and 180W to 180E. The global monthly anomaly product supersedes the former regional monthly anomaly datasets.

  Applications include drought and flood monitoring, agricultural assessment, water resource management, and environmental analysis.
  </Prose>
