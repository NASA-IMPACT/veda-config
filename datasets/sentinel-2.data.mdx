    ## Sentinel-2 for Disaster Monitoring

    This collection provides high-resolution multispectral imagery from the European Space Agency's Sentinel-2 satellites for disaster monitoring and environmental assessment. The data has been processed by NASA VEDA to provide specialized products including burn ratio indices for fire damage assessment and color infrared composites for vegetation monitoring.

    Sentinel-2 is a constellation of two polar-orbiting satellites (Sentinel-2A and Sentinel-2B) that provide high-resolution optical imagery with a revisit time of 5 days. The Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI) onboard captures imagery in 13 spectral bands from visible to shortwave infrared wavelengths.

    ## Normalized Burn Ratio (NBR)

    The Normalized Burn Ratio (NBR) is a spectral index calculated from Sentinel-2's near-infrared (NIR) and shortwave infrared (SWIR) bands as: `(NIR - SWIR) / (NIR + SWIR)`. Lower NBR values indicate greater burn damage intensity, while higher values represent healthy vegetation.

    ## Differenced Normalized Burn Ratio (dNBR)

    The Differenced Normalized Burn Ratio (dNBR) measures burn severity by comparing NBR values before and after a fire event: `NBR_prefire - NBR_postfire`. Higher dNBR values indicate more severe burn damage. Available at both daily and monthly temporal resolutions.

    ## Color Infrared Composite

    Color infrared (CIR) composites use the near-infrared band in combination with visible bands to create false-color imagery where vegetation appears in shades of red. Available at daily and subdaily temporal resolutions.
